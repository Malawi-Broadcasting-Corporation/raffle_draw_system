Check the entries, then lock the list

Locking freezes the entry list and records its fingerprint. After locking, no entry, prize or rule can change. Entries that arrive from the API later are kept on record but can't win.

Take the seed

The seed decides the winners together with the locked list. It must come from something nobody at MBC controls, and it can never be changed.

Public randomness (drand)

Uses the first drand round produced after the lock. Takes up to 30 seconds.

Manual seed

For example, roll a die 6 times on air, after the lock is shown and type the results.
